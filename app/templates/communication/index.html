Twilio SMS Gateway Configuration

Configure your Twilio account to enable automated SMS notifications for rent payments, arrears reminders, and booking confirmations.

Must be a verified Twilio phone number (in E.164 format) or an approved alphanumeric Sender ID.
When disabled or credentials are empty, all SMS dispatches are simulated and logged safely for auditing.
Send Test SMS

Verify your Twilio connection by sending an immediate test SMS to a mobile number.

Automated SMS Triggers
  • M-Pesa Rent Payments: Instant SMS receipt to tenant & alert to landlord.
  • Rent Invoices & Reminders: Arrears notices sent with account number.
  • Unit Reservations: Booking confirmation SMS to prospective tenants.
